D'Greek Cook (v1.2)

Playable Build ↗

Role: 3D Prop Artist · Timeline: October 2024 – May 2025 (10-Person Cross-Disciplinary Team)

🏆 GameJam+ Global 2024 Finalist · Miracle Academy Best Gameplay Nominee

The Challenge & My Role

Building D'Greek Cook—a fast-paced Greek mythology cooking game created by a 10-person team—meant solving a classic game-art challenge: making dozens of on-screen kitchen props look appealing while keeping performance lightweight and gameplay readable from a top-down camera. As the 3D Prop Artist, I took ownership of designing and modeling the game's core interactive properties in Blender, including geometric-patterned Greek plates, kitchen knives, wooden bowls, cutting boards, and mythological horned altars.

Technical Execution & Impact

Rather than relying on dense meshes, I prioritized strict topology optimization by minimizing vertex and face counts, removing redundant edge loops, and leveraging smooth shading to preserve clean, stylized silhouettes without taxing frame rates. I also maintained tight color-style consistency so players could quickly distinguish ingredients and utensils amidst hectic timer-based stages. This balance of aesthetic clarity and technical discipline helped propel our team to become a GameJam+ Global 2024 Finalist and a Best Gameplay Nominee at Miracle Academy.

Carnifall

Playable Build ↗

Role: 3D Prop & Weapon Artist · Timeline: July 2025 – August 2025 (10-Person Cross-Disciplinary Team)

🏆 Top 40 Student Category — GAMESEED 2025

The Challenge & My Role

Developed in a fast-paced two-month sprint for GAMESEED 2025 by a 10-person team, Carnifall blends third-person shooter action with tower defense mechanics inside a whimsical circus arena. As the 3D Prop Artist, I was responsible for building both hero combat/defense items—such as a stylized star revolver, harlequin mallet, sword, and theatrical mask totems—and atmospheric environment props including jack-in-the-box crates, treasure trunks, a popcorn cart, bunting banners, and giant patterned balloons in Blender.

Technical Execution & Impact

Because third-person cameras bring players close to weapons and defenses while enemy waves demand high frame rates, I tackled computational bottlenecks by crafting ultra-low-geometry meshes engineered to maintain a high-poly visual illusion. By pairing disciplined polygon reduction with smooth shading and bold stylized color blocking, every prop retained crisp silhouettes and rich surface detail without straining runtime performance—helping the team secure a Top 40 Student Category placement at GAMESEED 2025.

Squid Down

Playable Build ↗

Role: 3D Character & Prop Modeler · Timeline: November 2025 (GameJam+ Global 2025 Entry) · Tools: Blender

The Challenge & My Role

Created for GameJam+ Global 2025, Squid Down is a high-speed endless descender starring Lokie, a small octopus navigating past fans, platforms, and industrial pipes to set new depth records. Working in Blender and Houdini as the 3D Modeler, I took charge of modeling both the playable character (with multiple facial expressions) and the modular pipe obstacles that form the core hazards of the descent.

Technical Execution & Impact

In a fast-moving vertical game, players rely on split-second visual cues to dodge obstacles. To solve this, I deliberately contrasted Lokie's soft, organic silhouette against sharp-edged, dark metallic pipe structures so hazards read instantaneously on screen. Behind the scenes, I enforced clean low-poly topology and efficient UV mapping across every modular pipe piece—preventing memory spikes during endless obstacle spawning and keeping player maneuvering consistently smooth.

Baabaa & Beepow

Playable Build ↗

Role: 3D Character & Animation Artist · Timeline: June 2026 (GAMESEED 2026) · Tools: Blender, Unity

The Challenge & My Role

Baabaa & Beepow is a cooperative puzzle-platformer centered around an Alien and a Sheep whose signature mechanic allows the Alien to possess the Sheep to overcome obstacles. Stepping in as the 3D Artist (Modeling & Animation), I created both stylized character models and took ownership of the technical animation pipeline from Blender into Unity, where seamless state transitions were critical to making the possession mechanic feel responsive.

Technical Execution & Impact

Moving animated characters from a 3D tool into a game engine often exposes pipeline friction, and I tackled these bottlenecks head-on by troubleshooting and resolving FBX export–import issues—fixing broken rigs, missing meshes, and unrecognized animation clips. I then connected the Unity Animator Controller directly to the team's gameplay code, ensuring every character state and the core possession mechanic executed reliably in the live build.

Downtown Brew

Playable Build ↗

Context: Generative AI for Games Workshop · Stack: Unity (C#), Antigravity, Tripo AI

Developed for my Generative AI for Games Workshop (Workshop Kecerdasan Buatan Generatif Untuk Game), this project focused on critically analyzing the mechanics, workflow, and technical guardrails of using an AI Agent inside a Unity pipeline rather than relying on blind code generation. By pairing Antigravity for concept structuring and C# scripting with Tripo AI for 3D asset generation, I mapped out how the agent plans and executes multi-step tasks—maintaining strict human-in-the-loop oversight to prevent script context drift, broken Unity component bindings, and unoptimized 3D mesh topology. The result is a playable prototype paired with a clear technical methodology for integrating generative AI into game production safely and effectively.
